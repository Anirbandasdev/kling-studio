Kling Studio - please read this first

Installing

   Drag "Kling Studio" onto the Applications folder in this window.


"Kling Studio can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious
software"?

   That appears for any app that is not notarised by Apple, which this one is
   not. It does not mean anything is wrong with the app.

   To open it the first time:
      1. Find Kling Studio in your Applications folder.
      2. Right-click it (or Control-click) and choose Open.
      3. Click Open in the box that appears.

   You only have to do that once. After that it opens normally.

   If macOS refuses even then: System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll
   down, and click "Open Anyway" next to the message about Kling Studio.


What Kling Studio needs

   * A kie.ai account. The app asks for your API key the first time it opens,
     and keeps it on this Mac only.
   * Credits on that account: 12 for each frame, 90 for a 5-second video.
     The app always shows the total and asks before it spends anything.


Updates

   The app tells you when a new version is out and links to the download.
   On macOS, updating means downloading the new .dmg and dragging the app
   across again, replacing the old one.
